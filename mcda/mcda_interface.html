📋 Define Decision Structure

Alternatives (The Paths You're Considering)

What are alternatives? These are the specific options you're choosing between:
  • Houses: "Portland House", "Seattle House", "Vancouver House"
  • Job Offers: "Company A", "Company B", "Company C"
  • Locations: "Downtown Site", "Suburban Site", "Rural Site"
You will score how each alternative performs on your criteria.

Criteria (The Factors That Matter)

What are criteria? These are the factors you'll use to judge alternatives:
  • Objective: Measurable with data (Cost in USD, Distance in km, Time in hours)
  • Subjective: Requires judgment (Aesthetic appeal, Community fit, Resilience)
Subcriteria: Optional hierarchy for organization. Only leaf (bottom-level) criteria are weighted and scored.
Example: "Cost" → "Initial Investment", "Monthly Expenses", "Maintenance"

Objective Criteria - Enter Factual Data

📊 The Foundation of Facts: Objective criteria have measurable values that are the same for everyone. Enter them once here - they will automatically populate for all stakeholders.
Examples: Cost in USD, Distance in km, Time in hours
